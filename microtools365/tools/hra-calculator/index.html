HRA Exemption Calculator

Find out how much of your House Rent Allowance is tax-exempt under the old regime. This only applies if you've chosen the old tax regime — HRA exemption isn't available under the new regime.

—
HRA exemption (tax-free)

HRA exemption is the lowest of three amounts: actual HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of basic+DA, or 50%/40% of basic+DA (metro/non-metro). Whichever is smallest is what's exempt — the rest of your HRA is taxable as regular income.
