Uses a fixed withdrawal-rate approach: your corpus needs to be large enough that withdrawing the chosen percentage annually covers your inflation-adjusted expenses at retirement. A 4% withdrawal rate is a commonly cited starting point, though the right number for you depends on how long your retirement needs to last and how your money stays invested afterward. This is a simplified estimate, not personalized financial planning.
