Frequently Asked Questions & How-To Guide

How do I edit or replace existing text in a PDF?

Because PDF files are fixed printing coordinate streams rather than reflowable documents, direct text replacement is handled using the Whiteout & Add Text workflow: select the ⬜ Whiteout tool to draw an opaque box over the outdated text, then switch to the 📝 Add Text tool to type your new content directly on top. The result looks identical to native document editing.

Are my uploaded PDF files safe and confidential?

Yes, 100%. Unlike other PDF editors that upload your contracts, bank statements, or tax returns to external cloud servers, microtools365 processes everything locally in your browser runtime using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. Nothing is ever sent to or stored on our servers.

How do I add a signature or official stamp to a PDF?

You have two simple options: choose the ✍️ Pen tool to draw your handwritten signature directly on the document with your mouse or touchscreen, or click 🖼️ Add Image to place a pre-saved PNG of your signature, initial, or company stamp.

How does redaction work? Will my hidden data be truly secure?

When you use the ⬛ Redact tool, a solid, non-transparent vector box is baked directly into the output PDF stream over the selected area. This physically obscures and blocks the underlying coordinate region in the exported document.

Does this tool add a watermark to my edited PDF?

No. Your exported PDF contains no watermarks, branding, or restrictions. It is completely clean and ready for legal, professional, or academic use.
