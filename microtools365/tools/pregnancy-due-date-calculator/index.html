Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Estimate your due date and current week of pregnancy based on your last menstrual period (LMP).

This uses Naegele's rule (LMP + 280 days, adjusted for cycle length), the standard method for a first estimate. Only about 5% of babies arrive exactly on their due date — an ultrasound-based estimate from your doctor is more precise than this calculation alone. This tool is not a substitute for prenatal care.
